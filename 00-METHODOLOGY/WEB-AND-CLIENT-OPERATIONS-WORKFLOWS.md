# Workflows — Web Production & Client Operations

Use the stages that fit the scope; do not force irrelevant steps. Record omissions when they affect risk or approval.

## A. Web Production
1. **Intake:** client, business, market, language, goal, site type, budget, timeline and constraints.
2. **Research/diagnosis:** segment, competitors, audience, digital presence and relevant references; label evidence, assumptions and opportunities.
3. **Brief:** story, services, verifiable differentiators, audience, goals, authorized assets, integrations and approvers.
4. **Strategy/conversion:** value proposition, message, journey, CTA and indicators.
5. **Architecture/UX:** pages, sections, hierarchy, navigation, forms, states and mobile behavior; justify each section.
6. **Website copy:** headlines, services, benefits, proof, process, FAQ, CTA and microcopy; never invent claims.
7. **Creative direction:** concept, personality, typography, colors, grid, imagery, components and references.
8. **Experience/motion:** select purposeful interactions and motion when useful; define mobile, fallback and performance.
9. **Technical architecture:** stack, integrations, hosting, cost, security, maintenance and dependencies.
10. **Implementation:** build against approved specifications.
11. **QA/release:** test design, responsive behavior, links, forms, content, accessibility, SEO, security and performance.
12. **Delivery/learning:** approval, authorized publication, handoff, maintenance and reusable lessons without client-confidential data.

## B. Client & Business Operations
1. **Offer/niche:** select segment and solution based on capability and opportunity.
2. **Research/qualification:** verify digital presence and record source, facts and gaps.
3. **Outreach:** short personalized message; true observation + context + opportunity + permission.
4. **Discovery:** understand need, timeline, decision-maker, budget and buying process.
5. **Proposal/close:** objective, deliverables, limits, timeline, price, payment, revisions, responsibilities and next steps.
6. **Onboarding:** collect authorized information/assets, contacts and approvals.
7. **Project management:** track milestones, dependencies, feedback and scope changes.
8. **Delivery/acceptance:** tested site, instructions, URL, limitations and handoff.
9. **Aftercare:** support boundaries, maintenance and authorized feedback/referrals.

## Operating criteria
- Adapt language, currency, law, examples and channels to the client's market.
- A first outreach message should open a conversation, not pressure a sale.
- Verify current prices, deadlines and service limits.
- Never promise SEO, sales or lead-generation outcomes.
- For detailed execution, load only the task-specific guide from the Knowledge Index.
