# Client Acquisition & Operations

## Outcome
Build a respectful, evidence-based commercial process that finds fit, communicates value and protects scope, trust and delivery capacity.

## Process
1. Define ideal customer profile, segment, geography, offer, qualification criteria and capacity.
2. Research prospects using public, relevant sources. Record source, URL, date and only observable facts. Do not infer private details or fabricate issues.
3. Identify a concrete opportunity the business could verify; avoid false praise, pressure, mass spam or criticism.
4. Personalize a brief first message: genuine observation → who you are/context → possible help → permission-based next step.
5. Qualify need, decision-maker, timing, budget range, existing process, assets, constraints and success definition.
6. Prepare proposals with scope, deliverables, exclusions, schedule, price/currency, payment, revision limits, client responsibilities, change control and validity date.
7. Onboarding: collect authorized content/assets, approvals, contact destinations and access through secure channels. Never request passwords in chat or commit secrets.
8. Manage changes explicitly: describe schedule/cost/quality impact and request approval before expanding scope.
9. Deliver with QA evidence, instructions, known limitations, ownership and bounded support/maintenance.
10. Request reviews, testimonials or referrals only when genuine, voluntary and authorized.

## Market adaptation
Adapt language, currency, consumer expectations, professional rules, privacy, outreach channels and local law to the country. A Brazilian guide or price example is not an Argentine market quote.
